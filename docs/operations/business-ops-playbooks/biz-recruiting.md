- **Card id:** `biz-recruiting`
- **Human owner:** `va_plus_founder`
- **Primary artifact:** `recruiting-packet.md`
- **Done when:** Shortlist + rubric scores + recommended next interview steps; no auto-offer

## Inputs

- role need

## Steps

1. Draft role brief + score rubric (must/should/nice)
2. Write public role post draft (human gate before post)
3. Screen each candidate against rubric with evidence quotes
4. Produce shortlist of ≤5 with risks
5. Emit recruiting-packet.md; founder decides offers
