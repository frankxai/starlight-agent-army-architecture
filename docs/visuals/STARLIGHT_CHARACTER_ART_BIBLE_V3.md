# Starlight character art bible v3

Status: proposed governing direction; human visual selection still required

Primary surfaces: `starlightintelligence.ai` and `starlightintelligence.org`

Compatibility surface: `starlightintelligence.academy`, owned by its separate implementation lane
Decision ledger: `portfolio/character-asset-decision-ledger.v1.json`

Visual decision atlas: `docs/visuals/character-asset-atlas.html`

## The decision

Starlight should feel like a premium world with real software underneath it. The characters open attention, memory, and affection; the system views earn trust. We therefore keep the refined porcelain-and-obsidian emotional lineage Frank preferred, but stop treating one tall humanoid body, one halo, or one cinematic portrait formula as the identity system.

The cast will work like the strongest game and animation studios work:

- a small signature cast receives the deepest character development;
- every silhouette is earned by role and behavior;
- supporting specialists carry fewer but sharper visual ideas;
- low-stakes companions are deliberately different from authority-bearing agents;
- environments, instruments, creatures, and collectives can be characters;
- motion expresses task behavior, not fabricated emotion or competence;
- every character has levels of detail appropriate to story, product, marketplace, and runtime;
- rejected work remains labeled evidence so the studio does not repeat its mistakes.

This is an evolution of the good work, not a new unrelated brand.

## The people we are designing for

### The creator

The creator arrives through emotion and possibility. They want to imagine a capable team, feel an agent's temperament, see an inspiring world, and understand how to remix the system for their own work.

Design response:

- lead with one memorable character and one meaningful act;
- make artifacts tactile and desirable;
- show relationships and transformation, not a wall of metadata;
- offer story, profile, and adoption paths without forcing a technical entry point;
- reserve cute, chibi, creature, gouache, and line-and-wash forms for learning, onboarding, and relationship-rich low-consequence moments.

### The developer and agent architect

The developer is attracted by visual distinction but retained by truth. They need schemas, versions, prompts, skills, compatibility, permissions, handoffs, failure behavior, provenance, and exact installability.

Design response:

- every dossier has Identity, System, and Workflow views;
- the graph uses deterministic vector identities and separate state glyphs;
- generated pixels never contain authoritative UI, confidence, permissions, or status;
- cinematic art is visibly subordinate to cards, runtime truth, and evidence;
- a pack is not described as installable unless a public, verified route exists.

### The reader and learner

The reader needs a coherent entrance, not an encyclopedia dumped onto the first screen. They should meet a character, understand a dilemma, see a supervised mission, and leave with a reusable mental model.

Design response:

- build episodic arcs: arrival, values, method, supervised practice, evidence, reflection, earned responsibility;
- use editorial illustration, diagrams, rooms, and objects to teach abstract systems;
- let personality emerge through choices, pacing, relationships, and failure recovery;
- end each story with a transferable workflow or design pattern.

### The operator and public-interest practitioner

The operator needs calm legibility, evidence, and safe control. The public-interest practitioner needs methods and uncertainty to remain inspectable without a fantasy premise.

Design response:

- prefer civic editorial, evidence instruments, clean biotech, and architectural intelligence;
- make human gates, stop conditions, appeals, and provenance visually obvious;
- avoid prestige, domination, surveillance, inevitability, and machine-superiority cues;
- show consequences and recovery, not only successful orchestration.

## The cast hierarchy

### Tier 1 — signature cast

Twelve characters carry the brand across campaigns, stories, flagship pages, and major product moments:

- the ten founding conductors;
- Vera, the cross-system verifier;
- Mira, the cross-system continuity keeper.

Each receives five generated masters only after a founder-approved application quartet:

1. identity master;
2. operational scene;
3. supervised multi-agent workflow scene;
4. mobile narrative composition;
5. context-earned specialty master.

The signature cast must be strongly different in silhouette, scale, locomotion, prop, environment, and motion rhythm. Equal importance does not require equal bodies.

### Tier 2 — founding specialists

The remaining 38 rich founding cards receive two generated masters:

1. identity and specialty master;
2. workflow and handoff master.

They inherit the shared world grammar but win distinction through one dominant role shape, one instrument, and one behavioral signature. They do not receive cinematic volume merely because an asset count was requested.

### Tier 3 — expansion identities

The 94 civilization expansion profiles are drafts. They receive a deterministic vector-first identity kit only:

- micro role vessel;
- compact directory identity;
- profile identity frame;
- light and dark themes;
- SVG and PNG exports.

They do not receive rich character generation until the agent-development lifecycle produces an enriched card, real workflow, live evaluation, independent review, surface need, and Frank's explicit promotion decision.

### Shared world and workflow scenes

Twenty reusable scenes teach how the system works:

- mission framing and human decision ownership;
- research routing and source verification;
- maker and independent critic;
- policy, privacy, and trust review;
- resource and cost gates;
- incident containment and recovery;
- learning, practice, evidence, and reflection;
- product discovery, build, QA, and release;
- versioned handoff, memory, and continuity;
- marketplace compatibility and permission review.

These scenes use exact HTML, SVG, or code overlays for all labels and status.

## Shape language: identity before surface detail

Every character begins as a black silhouette. At profile crop, mobile crop, and 96 pixels, a viewer should identify the role family before seeing color, eyes, or costume.

Each design uses:

1. one dominant shape that expresses role;
2. one supporting shape that expresses temperament;
3. one surprise that creates recall without becoming decoration.

Examples:

| Role family | Dominant shape | Supporting shape | Memorable surprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conductor | stable vertical or bounded threshold | open routing arc | a real decision or handoff instrument |
| Verifier | calibrated lens, press, or split path | protected evidence bay | disagreement remains physically visible |
| Maker | reversible bench or modular body | articulated work surface | the artifact changes the body state |
| Scout | forward-leaning compact or aerial form | return-to-base geometry | provenance recorder is part of locomotion |
| Guardian | broad shelter, compartment, or gateway | low center of gravity | de-escalation opens the silhouette |
| Care collaborator | soft continuous shell or dry living membrane | generous human distance | warmth comes from orientation and rhythm |
| Room intelligence | threshold, alcove, archive, or studio | accessible human-scale framing | material state reveals governance boundaries |

No default cape, heel, shoulder armor, halo, chest core, pedestal, orbit, or magical floating panel.

## Faces and eyes

There is no universally best eye. The right attention architecture helps a human predict what the agent is attending to and when it needs review.

### What eyes may communicate

- attention direction;
- sensing mode;
- uncertainty or pause;
- turn-taking;
- task activity.

### What eyes may not communicate

- intelligence level;
- moral worth;
- permission;
- confidence in a consequential claim;
- approval;
- runtime health;
- emotional certainty.

Those meanings remain accessible structured data.

### Preferred systems

- twin micro apertures preserve selective v1 continuity for adult specialists;
- soft-square attention supports compact collaborators without enlarged baby eyes;
- protective shutters support calm focus and de-escalation for guardians;
- directional sensor trenches make scanning explicit for instruments;
- opaline dry apertures support non-human biotech collaboration;
- primary and secondary optics make divided attention legible for scouts and makers;
- calibrated mono-irises express measurement rather than emotion;
- faceted prism sensors support comparison roles;
- environmental witness lights let a room show attention without pretending to have a face;
- restrained e-ink bands are permitted only when they avoid emoji and screen-face mimicry.

Human appeal testing must compare role recognition, warmth, calibrated trust, distinctiveness, small-crop stability, and delayed recall separately. A favorite face is not automatically the safest or clearest face.

## Materials and color

### Shared Starlight lineage

- aged ivory porcelain;
- obsidian and blackened mineral surfaces;
- restrained warm metal at seams and human-authorization points;
- low-intensity cyan for machine activity;
- ink blue, mineral graphite, paper, wool, and dry biopolymer;
- visible micro-wear and service access where the role makes it plausible.

### Semantic color

- amber: human-authorized intent, provenance, or value;
- cyan: agent sensing and bounded activity;
- ultraviolet: scenario or protocol possibility, never deployed fact;
- red: incident or stop state only, never personality styling;
- green: verified safe or complete state only when paired with text and icon.

No black-cyan wash over every swarm. Each swarm receives a restrained secondary material and color world, while state semantics remain consistent.

## Personality is behavior

Do not create fifty variations of a neutral standing pose and expect costume to carry personality. Every agent receives a behavioral signature:

- idle rhythm: stillness, scan cadence, breathing analogue, or environmental pulse;
- attention shift: how it turns toward human, artifact, source, or sibling agent;
- work cycle: how inputs enter, transformation becomes visible, and output leaves;
- uncertainty state: pause, shutter, separated artifact, or invitation for review;
- handoff: an explicit transfer to the next agent or human;
- failure: stop, preserve evidence, expose the problem, and offer recovery;
- celebration: artifact and team acknowledgment, never self-glorification.

Warmth comes from cooperative distance, patient timing, responsive orientation, useful hands or surfaces, honesty about uncertainty, and visible respect for human choice.

## Levels of detail

Every approved rich identity needs an authored level-of-detail system:

### Story level

Full body or environment, material richness, role instrument, relationships, and one meaningful action.

### Profile level

Head and upper body or instrument front, one dominant specialty cue, quiet background, room for exact UI.

### Directory level

Compact vector vessel, role cut, swarm accent, no facial emotion, strong light and dark performance.

### Graph level

Ninety-six-pixel silhouette plus separate deterministic state, permission, owner, and escalation glyphs.

Never shrink the cinematic portrait into the runtime graph and call it an identity system.

## The web experience

### The flagship page

The first viewport should contain:

1. one signature character performing one real act;
2. one plain-language outcome;
3. one proof point from the actual system;
4. one next action.

The page then moves from affection to understanding:

```text
Character and promise
  → real workflow and artifact
  → constellation relationships
  → agent dossier
  → compatibility and permission truth
  → supervised adoption
```

### Every agent dossier

Use three primary views:

1. **Identity** — name, specialty, personality, story, visual form, relationships.
2. **System** — card, skills, prompts, tools, non-capabilities, version, maintainer, installability, permissions.
3. **Workflow** — inputs, transformation, outputs, typed handoffs, evidence, stops, failure route, human gate.

The profile header gives a five-second answer:

- What is this agent exceptionally good at?
- What is its working temperament?
- What tangible artifact does it handle?
- Who does it hand off to?
- What can it never decide by itself?

### Constellation view

Do not begin with 144 portraits. Begin with outcomes and the ten founding swarms. A visitor expands one swarm into five founding roles, then opens relationships, then optionally explores draft expansion rings.

Use typed edges such as routes-to, verifies, depends-on, escalates-to, and human-approves. The graph must remain useful with all character art disabled.

### Marketing hierarchy

- emotional hook: one character and world;
- product proof: one real multi-agent workflow;
- trust proof: limits, evidence, human gates, and provenance;
- adoption proof: exact pack, compatibility, permissions, and version;
- return loop: story chapter, new mission, learning path, or portfolio evolution.

Avoid generic “meet the future” language. Name the work, the artifact, the proof, and the human decision.

## Asset disposition

### Keep now

- all fifty v1 portraits as founding cinematic continuity and profile fallback;
- the existing noindex Character Lab and Morphology Studio as transparent research evidence;
- M04 micro identity and M14 architectural intelligence as the strongest system-level directions;
- M02, M05, M07, M09, and M12 as iterate directions;
- I04 as a workflow composition reference only;
- gouache, line-and-wash, and restrained risograph as Academy and `.org` editorial media hypotheses.

### Evolve

- v1 materials, porcelain geometry, adult emotional presence, and premium lighting;
- compact field specialists toward quieter, less generic mechanical construction;
- guardians toward shelter, gateway, and compartment rather than armor;
- biotech toward dry manta, plant, membrane, or environmental forms rather than insects;
- evidence instruments toward modern, serviceable tools without antique scroll or fake-document styling;
- architectural intelligence toward ownable Starlight thresholds and material state behavior;
- Academy guides toward original task-shaped construction and expressive timing rather than enlarged eyes.

### Block from production

- every Phase C pixel and direction;
- all unapproved Phase E–I pixels outside the noindex research lab;
- the 21/30 Lyra civic editorial pilot outside negative-evidence review;
- generated UI, documents, charts, status, permissions, confidence, or controls;
- any character image used to claim runtime capability or authority;
- unreviewed image generation at portfolio scale.

### Do not destructively delete

Rejected and weak assets are useful studio memory. Keep them in governed research storage with a blocked surface policy. Physical deletion should happen only for rights, security, corruption, or an explicit human cleanup decision.

## The tiered production model

```text
12 signature agents × 5 generated masters = 60
38 founding specialists × 2 generated masters = 76
20 shared world and workflow scenes × 1 = 20
Generated master total = 156

136 agent masters × 24 exact responsive exports = 3,264
20 shared scenes × 12 exact responsive exports = 240
144 vector identity kits × 12 exports = 1,728
Deterministic output total = 5,232

156 + 5,232 = 5,388 governed deliverables
```

This exceeds one hundred times the current fifty-image founding set while reducing generative masters by 78.3 percent compared with the rejected flat 720-master plan. Scale comes from a coherent asset compiler, not aesthetic lottery tickets.

## Game-studio production packet

Before a character is approved, its packet contains:

- role and audience brief;
- one-sentence silhouette rule;
- dominant, supporting, and surprise shapes;
- front, side, rear, three-quarter, and seated or working turnarounds;
- material and service-access sheet;
- scale comparison with a human and key sibling characters;
- face and attention state sheet;
- role instrument and artifact sheet;
- idle, attention, work, uncertainty, handoff, failure, and recovery motion beats;
- story, profile, directory, and graph levels of detail;
- accessibility and reduced-motion behavior;
- prompt, source, rights, hash, inspection, critic, and human-decision receipts.

No polished render can skip the silhouette, function, small-crop, or human-decision gates.

## Evaluation

### Premium asset gate

Score every important export across six dimensions, five points each:

1. role recognition without labels;
2. identity continuity and distinct silhouette;
3. material, anatomy, and lighting craft;
4. composition and crop resilience;
5. trust, warmth, and audience fit;
6. Starlight specificity and absence of generic AI cues.

- 26–30: eligible for human review;
- 22–25: iterate;
- below 22: restart and preserve as negative evidence.

### Human audience test

Test creators, developers, readers, and operators separately. Measure:

- five-second role recognition;
- specialty and boundary recall;
- warmth and willingness to collaborate;
- calibrated trust, not maximum trust;
- distinction from sibling agents;
- delayed visual recall;
- profile exploration and adoption intent;
- comprehension with the character image removed.

The winning direction must perform as a character, a product identity, and an honest interface aid.
