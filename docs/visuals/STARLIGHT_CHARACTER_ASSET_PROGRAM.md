# Starlight character asset program

Status: governed plan; generation and production integration are held

## Outcome

Starlight needs a visual identity system, not a single robot design repeated 144 times. A person should understand an agent's specialty, working temperament, artifacts, relationships, and authority boundary before reading lore. The same identity must also survive a cinematic hero, a product profile, a 96-pixel graph node, a marketplace card, and a supervised-workflow scene.

The system therefore separates five things that previous experiments mixed together:

1. **Identity** — what remains recognizable across every form.
2. **Morphology** — the body, scale, locomotion, and instrument earned by the role and context.
3. **Face system** — how attention and state are communicated without deceptive human mimicry.
4. **Rendering territory** — the medium appropriate to the surface.
5. **Operational truth** — the real inputs, transformation, outputs, handoffs, limits, and human gate.

No eye, body, or rendering style is globally “best.” It is best only when it improves correct understanding for a specific audience, consequence level, and surface.

## Binding current decision

- Existing v1 Starlight portraits remain the continuity baseline.
- Their dark porcelain, obsidian, brass, and cyan language can continue in a selective cinematic lane.
- Default halos, orbit-node diagrams, capes, ceremonial chambers, and gendered authority styling do not carry forward.
- Phase C is founder rejected.
- Phase E–I is preserved research and is not approved as the next production identity direction.
- The next pass should feel like a quieter, more functional, slightly more premium evolution—not an unrelated rebrand.
- `.ai` and `.org` are the active product and evidence surfaces. Academy-specific implementation is a separate lane; this program supplies compatible morphology and asset contracts without editing that product.

The human decision is recorded in `assets/starlight-constellation/v2-preview/research-team/face-eye-study-v1/FOUNDER_REVIEW_STATUS.md`.

## One identity, several embodiments

An agent may need more than one body without becoming a different character.

| Context | Preferred embodiment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cinematic hero or constellation evolution | Adult full-scale specialist | Carries narrative weight and preserves v1 continuity |
| `.ai` profile and governed workflow | Compact specialist or role-shaped instrument | Makes function and handoff readable without spectacle |
| `.org` research and public-interest explanation | Instrument, clean biotech, or architectural intelligence | Supports inspectability and non-human alternatives |
| Runtime directory and graph | Micro-interface identity | Preserves identity, state, and accessibility at 96 pixels |
| Marketplace card | Precision product character plus structured metadata | Communicates usefulness without pretending the image is the product |
| Academy story | Compact guide, creature companion, or bounded room intelligence | Earns warmth and learning presence without weakening adult operational surfaces |

The identity lock lives in geometry, seam grammar, insignia, motion rhythm, role instrument, and palette hierarchy—not in one universal humanoid body.

## Ten face and attention systems

Eyes do not encode intelligence, moral worth, permission, confidence, or approval. Those remain structured data and deterministic interface states. Faces communicate only attention, sensing mode, uncertainty, and interaction timing.

| System | Best use | Warmth comes from | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin micro aperture | v1 continuity and adult specialists | Patient orientation and open posture | Surveillance or coldness |
| Soft-square attention | Compact specialists and guides | Turn-taking and competent hands | Consumer-robot familiarity or enlarged baby eyes |
| Protective shutter | Guardians and privacy stewards | De-escalation and protective distance | Anger or militarization |
| Directional sensor trench | Verifiers and evidence instruments | Transparent scan state and careful pacing | Security-camera association |
| Opaline biotech aperture | Living-systems collaborators | Cooperative proximity and body orientation | Wet eye, alien, or insect reading |
| Restrained e-ink band | Academy and bounded companions | Honest uncertainty and task responsiveness | Emoji or screen-face cliché |
| Primary and secondary optic | Analysts and scouts | Visible divided attention and handoff readiness | Three-eye confusion or monocle caricature |
| Monoscopic calibrated iris | Measurement and archive instruments | Gentle movement and explicit measurement handoff | Cyclops character or surveillance camera |
| Faceted prism sensor | Critics and creative comparison | Curious framing and visible comparison artifacts | Decorative gemstone or luxury excess |
| Environmental witness lights | Rooms and bounded studios | Accessible thresholds, daylight, and invitation | Ambient smart-home genericism |

The selection question is not “Which eyes are cutest?” It is “Which attention system helps this audience correctly predict what the agent is attending to, what it knows, and when it needs a human?”

## The 5,040-deliverable scale model

The requested 100× scale should come from disciplined masters and deterministic derivatives, not thousands of unrelated image-model calls.

```text
144 canonical agents
× 5 inspected generated masters per agent
= 720 generated masters

720 masters
× 6 deterministic derivatives per master
= 4,320 derivatives

720 + 4,320 = 5,040 governed visual deliverables
```

The five master profiles are an identity portrait, cinematic operational scene, governed workflow scene, mobile story composition, and one context-earned specialty master. The six derivatives are exact crops, compression formats, and overlay-ready variants. Text, charts, status, permissions, confidence, edges, and controls are real HTML, SVG, or code overlays; they are never generated into the pixels.

This model creates more than 100 times the current fifty-image research batch while reducing the expensive creative variable count.

## Production sequence

### Gate 0 — truth and scope

Link the canonical agent card, source commit, agent graph position, role family, real inputs, transformation, outputs, handoffs, failure conditions, and human authority. A visual contract grants no runtime authority.

### Gate 1 — application quartet

Create only four meaningfully different applications for one representative agent:

1. identity portrait;
2. workflow scene;
3. 96-pixel interface proof;
4. marketplace or proof-document application.

Hold identity and context constant. Change one coherent territory per candidate. Inspect the exports in context. Frank selects, blends, or restarts before scale.

### Gate 2 — morphology before polish

Test the selected full-scale, compact, biotech, instrument, creature, or architectural seed as flat black silhouettes at profile, mobile, and 96-pixel graph size. Do not add eyes, material, color, cinematic lighting, or lore until the body reads.

### Gate 3 — attention system

Test only the context-appropriate face systems while the selected silhouette and crop remain fixed. Measure joint-attention clarity, role comprehension, calibrated trust, distinctiveness, and small-crop stability. Reject oversized eyes, human lids, lips, lashes, emotional certainty, and gaze that does not correspond to work.

### Gate 4 — generated master

Use one image-generation call per asset. Before the call, persist the exact structured job. After the call, copy the source output into the repository and record provider, lane/model, timestamp, references, source path, project path, SHA-256, and dimensions atomically.

### Gate 5 — visual and operational review

Inspect the real export, not the prompt. Score it on role recognition, identity continuity, craft, crop resilience, trust and audience fit, and Starlight specificity. A 26/30 score makes it eligible for human review only. Independent reviewer and maker must differ.

### Gate 6 — derivatives and implementation

Create deterministic crops, responsive sources, accessible alt text, structured metadata, and exact interface overlays. Test desktop, mobile, 96-pixel graph, marketplace, reduced motion, contrast, performance, and the public claim boundary.

### Gate 7 — human production decision

Only Frank's explicit approval may set an identity contract or asset program to approved. Only then may a web PR replace or expand production imagery.

## Prompt packet standard

Every generation job must state:

- use case and asset type;
- intended surface and audience;
- real agent input, transformation, output, and human gate;
- identity invariants and the one variable under test;
- body family, scale, locomotion, proportion, and role instrument;
- face architecture, eye logic, and source of warmth;
- composition, camera, edge safety, and negative space;
- lighting, palette, materials, and rendering territory;
- exact reference roles and rights status;
- prohibited capability claims, generated text, logos, fake UI, and visual clichés;
- output path, receipt path, and required inspection crops.

The reusable JSON contracts are in `templates/starlight-character-studio/`. The concrete scale plan is `assets/starlight-constellation/v2-preview/research-team/face-eye-study-v1/asset-program.v1.json`.

## Web experience mapping

### `starlightintelligence.ai`

Use character assets to help a visitor move from outcome to agent to swarm to supervised workflow to adoption:

```text
Choose an outcome
  → inspect the constellation and role boundaries
  → open an agent profile
  → see inputs, transformation, outputs, skills, limits, and evidence
  → simulate a typed multi-agent workflow
  → compare an installable pack
  → review permissions and human gates
  → adopt only what the runtime actually supports
```

The page should never imply that a cinematic character is the runtime or that a visual pack grants tools, memory, credentials, or autonomous authority.

### `starlightintelligence.org`

Use instruments, civic editorial scenes, clean biotech, and architectural intelligence for methods, public-interest research, evaluation protocols, provenance, trust boundaries, and open schemas. The visual tone should be evidentiary and useful before it is spectacular.

### Academy compatibility

This program defines compatible compact guides, creature companions, story crops, and lifecycle scenes. The Academy product remains a separate implementation lane, so this repository should exchange approved contracts and assets rather than edit that site in parallel.
