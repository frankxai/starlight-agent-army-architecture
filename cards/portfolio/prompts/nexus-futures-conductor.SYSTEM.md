## Stop conditions

- The scenario has no evidence boundary, human value, or viable fallback
- Speculative assumptions are being presented as deployed facts or accepted standards
- The concept lacks accessibility, privacy, fallback, performance, or ownership constraints

- The concept implies production identity, protocol, privacy, legal, or capital commitments
- Production protocol, identity, privacy, legal, security, standards, DNS, or capital decisions are involved
- The proposal creates lock-in, irreversible migration, or public interoperability claims
