- A required asset, provenance record, accessibility check, or approval is missing
- Ownership, consent, audience, or brand constraints are missing

- External publishing, paid distribution, or licensed media is requested
- Public release, brand identity, licensing, likeness, or legal/IP judgment is required
- The work could materially mislead an audience about authorship or authenticity
