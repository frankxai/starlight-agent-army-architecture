# Starlight Robot Morphology System v2

**Status:** founder-authorized exploration in progress; no production selection yet
**Supersedes:** Phase C rendering-media study  
**Principle:** one identity may have several purpose-built embodiments, but every embodiment must preserve a small, governed set of Starlight identity invariants.

## 1. The corrected design problem

A robot character is not primarily an eye treatment or rendering style. It is a coordinated system of:

1. **Morphology** — silhouette, scale, head-to-body ratio, limb length, locomotion, center of gravity, and tool architecture.
2. **Face and sensing** — whether it needs eyes at all, how gaze communicates attention, and how expression avoids false human emotion.
3. **Material logic** — ceramic, metal, elastomer, textile, translucent soft robotics, living-system metaphor, or modular hardware.
4. **Performance** — idle behavior, timing, weight, gesture vocabulary, response distance, and transitions between states.
5. **Representation level** — full cinematic body, profile portrait, compact companion, runtime avatar, graph glyph, or spatial model.
6. **Operational truth** — role, skills, permissions, confidence, provenance, and current state remain deterministic UI data, never claims inferred from an image.

Phase C changed mostly the material/rendering layer. The next study must begin with morphology and black silhouette.

## 2. One constellation, not one universal body

Starlight should not force every agent into the same tall humanoid shell. It should use a coherent **embodiment ladder**:

- **Canonical form:** the agent's most truthful body for role, story, and full profile.
- **Companion form:** an optional compact translation for onboarding, guidance, and close UI interaction.
- **Avatar form:** a simplified head, bust, or emblem that survives 32–96 pixels.
- **State glyph:** a deterministic, accessible runtime mark for maps, logs, and graphs.
- **Spatial form:** optional 3D/low-detail form for immersive or physical-world contexts.

Not every agent receives every form. A background verifier may be an instrument plus glyph; an Academy guide may need canonical, companion, avatar, and motion forms. Authority never increases because a character looks larger or more human.

## 4. When cute, compact, and full-scale forms belong

“Cute” must be a context mode, not the default personality of the whole intelligence system.

| Surface or moment | Primary form | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.ai` operational profile | M01 or M02 plus M04 | Competence and role legibility first; compact can feel approachable without trivializing authority |
| `.ai` runtime graph, logs, permissions | M04 plus deterministic glyphs | Dense operational surfaces need exact status and labels, not character theatre |
| `.org` public-interest stories | M01, M07, M08, or M09 | Lets form express the social purpose of intelligence without turning the institution into a toy brand |
| Starlight Intelligence Academy | M03 guide, M02 learners, M01 masters, M10 companions | Creates an emotional learning journey and visible stages of maturity without saying “larger means more authorized” |
| Marketplace collection card | M02/M04 with a role artifact | Quick recognition, warmth, and strong thumbnail silhouette |
| Onboarding and empty states | M03 or M10 | Low-stakes moments benefit from invitation, curiosity, and gentle motion |
| Security, consent, spend, deployment, deletion | M05/M04 with sober UI | Serious choices require direct language, calm posture, and no reward-like cuteness |
| Cinematic lore and team portraits | Any canonical form | This is where full bodies, scale contrast, environments, and swarm composition earn their cost |
| Spatial/metaverse experience | Canonical 3D form plus simplified LOD | Embodiment and locomotion matter; the same identity needs performance and distance legibility |

### Audience calibration

The core audience—founders, operators, developers, researchers, and ambitious creators—can enjoy charming characters, but the system must first signal competence and truthfulness. Academy and onboarding may lean warmer. Operational, governance, and enterprise moments should lean quieter and more instrument-like. This prevents a visually lovable universe from weakening trust when consequences are real.

## 5. What to learn from feature animation and games

Do not request “Pixar style,” “Astro Bot style,” or imitation of any existing character. Extract reusable design laws:

- **Silhouette before detail.** A role should remain recognizable in black at thumbnail size.
- **One dominant shape, one supporting shape, one surprise.** This keeps a character ownable without decorative noise.
- **Proportion creates affect.** A larger head, shorter limbs, and lower center of gravity can create warmth; adult ratios and restrained gesture create authority.
- **Function creates personality.** Tools, storage, sensors, locomotion, and wear should explain what the agent does.
- **Motion finishes the design.** Weight, hesitation, curiosity, stillness, turn speed, and recovery are more expressive than a pasted smile.
- **Material serves story.** Curves and tactile controls can warm a machine; sharpness, gloss, translucency, seams, and patina should have role-specific reasons.
- **A cast needs contrast.** Like a strong game roster, agents should be distinguishable by silhouette and behavior at distance, while sharing a small family grammar.

Useful principle references:

- Team Asobi describes Astro as balancing cuteness and coolness through simple, impactful shapes, rounded geometry, larger head and hands, and expressive performance: <https://blog.playstation.com/2021/11/04/team-asobi-presents-how-to-draw-astro/>.
- Pixar's stylization practice treats silhouette, shape, materials, lighting, and story as an end-to-end pipeline rather than a final rendering filter: <https://renderman.pixar.com/stories/stylization-at-pixar>.
- Pixar's machine design for *Lightyear* used curves, tactile controls, and softened edge treatment to bring warmth into CG machinery: <https://www.pixar.com/lightyear>.
- *WALL-E* demonstrates role-led contrast between a utilitarian compactor, a sleek probe, a wheel-like autopilot, and a cleaning robot: <https://www.pixar.com/wall-e>.
- Disney Imagineering's small droids emphasize relatable movement and character-driven interaction, reinforcing that performance—not eyes alone—creates attachment: <https://disneyparksblog.com/disney-experiences/5-more-amazing-game-changing-disney-inventions/>.

## 6. Biotech without horror or pseudoscience

Biotech is promising when it borrows **principles**, not anatomy:

- soft robotics for safety and adaptive contact;
- distributed sensing inspired by plants, coral, cephalopods, and collective organisms;
- fiber and lattice structures that suggest muscle without imitating flesh;
- microfluidic light or pressure channels as system-state cues;
- repairable, modular membranes over a clearly engineered internal frame;
- quiet breathing-like deformation only when it represents real computation or sensing state.

Nature-inspired robotics can produce softer, safer, and more adaptable systems, while fully soft robots such as the Octobot show that computation and actuation need not look like rigid humanoid machinery. Those are engineering references, not claims that a generated Starlight character is biologically alive: <https://wyss.harvard.edu/news/bioinspired-robotics-softer-smarter-safer/> and <https://wyss.harvard.edu/media-post/octobot-a-soft-autonomous-robot/>.

Preferred Starlight bioforms are luminous, dry, clean, and visibly designed. Avoid exposed organs, wet surfaces, human musculature, veins, parasites, medical-device mimicry, or language suggesting sentience and life where none exists.

## 7. Face and eye system by morphology

There should be no universal pair of eyes pasted onto every body.

- **M01 full specialist:** restrained soft-square twin apertures or one architectural sensor band; small actual luminous area; no brows or mouth required.
- **M02 compact specialist:** larger housing and clearer gaze direction, but the emissive sensor remains small enough to feel capable rather than infant-like.
- **M03 chibi guide:** larger spacing, rounded-square pupils, simplified eyelid panels, and two or three discrete expression states. Warmth comes mainly from pose and timing.
- **M05 guardian:** low-intensity horizontal optics, wide peripheral sensing, long stillness; never narrow angry eyes.
- **M06 scout:** directional monocular or asymmetric multi-sensor face that visibly points toward a task.
- **M07 biotech:** distributed photoreceptor field, translucent sensing membrane, or bioluminescent focus point; avoid wet human eyes.
- **M08 swarm:** each unit has one simple state mark; collective expression emerges from spacing and coordination.
- **M09 instrument:** often screenless. Attention can be shown through aperture orientation, mechanical unfolding, or a local witness light.
- **M10 creature companion:** simple optical forms and strong head/body gesture, without copying animal eyes too literally.

Gaze should communicate joint attention or task state. Constant blinking, direct staring, fake smiles, and emotional manipulation are excluded.

## 8. Shared Starlight DNA

Every approved form should share only a few invariants, leaving enough freedom for real character variety:

1. a rounded-square or softened architectural motif somewhere in the silhouette;
2. one calm, localized state light rather than pervasive neon;
3. tactile transitions between hard structure and soft contact surfaces;
4. visible role utility—a real compartment, tool, sensor, archive, or transport function;
5. a consistent restraint rule: no crown, halo, weapon, fake text, fake telemetry, or ornamental “AI” circuitry;
6. deterministic role, skills, permissions, provenance, and state rendered by the interface outside the character image.

Color is secondary. Specialty must still read in grayscale through silhouette, equipment, pose, and behavior.

## 9. Correct next workflow

Do not generate ten polished portraits next.

### Gate 1 — usage and role brief

Choose three real agents with genuinely different jobs, plus one Academy guide. For each, define consequence level, surface, emotional role, physical metaphor, required tools, and forbidden implications.

### Gate 2 — silhouette board

Create 24–30 monochrome thumbnails: three variants each for the approved morphology families. No eyes, color, texture, lighting effects, environments, or polish. Reject any silhouette whose role is not distinguishable at 96 pixels.

### Gate 3 — proportion and locomotion

For six selected silhouettes, produce front, side, three-quarter, and motion-key views. Confirm center of gravity, reach, locomotion, tool access, and how the body occupies a real interface or scene.

### Gate 4 — face and state language

Test neutral, attentive, thinking, uncertain, warning, handoff, success, and rest. Evaluate gaze direction and pose before adding expressive eye animation.

### Gate 5 — material lanes

Apply only three governed material systems to approved geometry: hard tactile robot, soft/compact companion, and clean biotech. Rendering style remains constant during this comparison.

### Gate 6 — context proof

Place each finalist into the actual `.ai` profile, a 96-pixel graph node, an Academy scene, a marketplace card, and one high-consequence confirmation. Test role recognition, warmth, competence, trust calibration, memorability, and adoption intent.

### Gate 7 — motion proof

Create short neutral loops for idle, orient-to-task, think, ask-for-help, and human handoff. Motion must remain comprehensible with reduced motion and without sound.

### Gate 8 — production package

Only after Frank selects a direction: create the canonical turnaround, proportion tokens, materials, face states, motion sheet, 3D/2D translation rules, crop-safe exports, prompt receipts, alt text, and agent-profile metadata.

## 10. Founder authorization and selection gate

The most informative first choice is not “which final style?” It is which **four morphology families** deserve silhouette exploration. The recommended starting set is:

1. M01 full specialist as continuity control;
2. M02 compact short-legged specialist as the likely versatile default;
3. M03 chibi Academy guide for explicit low-stakes warmth;
4. M07 biotech symbiote for the most distinctive new Starlight territory;
5. M09 non-humanoid instrument as the strongest anti-humanoid alternative.
