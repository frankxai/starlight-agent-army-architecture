# Independent Phase E–I visual critique

Audit date: 2026-08-28

Scope: 50 PNG research candidates

Reviewer lane: independent provenance and visual critique; no participation in generation or pixel editing

## Verdict

None of the 50 assets is production approved. Forty-nine score below the 26/30 human-review threshold. I04 reaches 26/30 because it shows a real supervised workflow, distinct agent roles, shared artifacts, and a human gate; it still remains a research candidate because the exact prompt, model, timestamp, crop proof, live-surface QA, and founder approval are missing.

The set is visually polished, but polish is not the same as a trustworthy Starlight identity system. The strongest path is to keep differentiated morphology and workflow storytelling while removing baby-schema, arachnid, gendered authority, fake-interface, orbit/node, and ceremonial-luxury defaults.

## Phase E — canonical morphology family

Strongest: E07 aerial knowledge navigator, E08 modular fabricator, E02 compact field operator. The family demonstrates useful embodiment breadth, but spider anatomy, baby-schema eyes, ceremonial pedestals, orbit props, and weak role legibility prevent canon lock.

## Phase F — face and eye systems

Strongest: F05 protective shutters, F06 opaline living apertures, F08 vertical sensor trench. F01, F07, and F10 drift toward gendered humanoid beauty; F04 and F09 become consumer-mascot faces. Warmth should come from attention, timing, and posture rather than wet-looking human eyes or smiles.

## Phase G — rendering languages

Strongest: G05 gouache, G06 line-and-wash, G07 risograph. The phase identifies useful context styles, but it repeats the same childlike guide morphology. Treat these as medium hypotheses, not a canonical character decision.

## Phase H — ring stewards

Strongest: H05 media/energy/resilience, H07 planetary observation, H09 infrastructure/operations. Several portraits use ceremonial authority, fake UI, and broad multi-domain props instead of one observable workflow. H06 should restart.

## Phase I — lifecycle story

I04 is the only 26/30 diagnostic and the clearest expression of real multi-agent coordination. I01–I03 offer a useful narrative sequence but lean on newborn metaphors and fake interfaces; I05 returns to the orbit/node spectacle the design standards warn against.

## Governance requirement

Future image-generation calls must persist the exact prompt or edit instruction, tool and model, timestamp, input references, source output path, project copy path, and SHA-256 atomically. A filename-derived intent summary is not a prompt receipt. Without that atomic record, an asset cannot advance beyond research even when its diagnostic score reaches 26/30.

## Best resumable next step

Use I04 as the composition reference for a controlled second pass: replace every generated interface element with deterministic overlays, label the maker–verifier–human-gate sequence, add a visible failure/rollback route, and prove desktop and mobile crops. In parallel, carry F05/F08 eye logic and E07/H09 role-specific morphology into three adult, non-arachnid character tests.
