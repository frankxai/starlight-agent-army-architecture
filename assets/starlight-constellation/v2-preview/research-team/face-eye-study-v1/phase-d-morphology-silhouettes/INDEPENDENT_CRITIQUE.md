## Verdict

The study contains several excellent ideas, but it does not pass its stated silhouette gate. Thirteen of fourteen boards are polished grayscale renders rather than flat silhouette studies. Actual 96-pixel recognition, real UI crops, accessibility, locomotion, and mechanical proofs remain untested.

- Keep as direction: M04 micro avatar, M14 room intelligence.
- Targeted iteration: M02 compact specialist, M03 Academy guide, M05 guardian, M07 biotech, M09 instrument, M12 fabricator.
- Restart board while preserving named seeds: M01 full specialist, M06 scout, M08 swarm, M10 companion, M11 care collaborator, M13 aerial navigator.
