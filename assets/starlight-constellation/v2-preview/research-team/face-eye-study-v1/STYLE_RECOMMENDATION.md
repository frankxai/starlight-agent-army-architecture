# Starlight character style recommendation

This is a controlled maker diagnosis, not identity approval.

## Recommendation

Do not force one visual medium to perform every job. Keep one stable character geometry and use a governed family of media by surface:

| Surface | Primary direction | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.ai` operational profiles | C02 precision pen and wash | Clearest balance of technical credibility, calm humanity, documentation fit, and repeatability. |
| `.org` public trust and essays | C01 civic editorial gouache | Warmest mature expression; feels authored and public-minded rather than like generic AI product art. |
| Academy subject profiles | C05 scientific naturalist plate | Reads as inspectable knowledge and supports archive, curriculum, and specimen metaphors. |
| Academy workflow lessons | C06 architectural cutaway, redesigned from canonical primitives | Makes skills and internal workflow visible; requires factual, reusable component grammar before production. |
| Marketplace and campaign tiles | C07 linocut or C03 modernist screenprint | Strongest small-crop recognition and most ownable collection behavior. |
| Narrative scenes and motion | Revised C08 animation language or C10 physical maquette | Adds emotional accessibility without redesigning the face or using simulated human expression. |

## Founder decision set

The three most useful directions to place into a real agent-profile UI are:

1. C01 civic editorial gouache — strongest predicted human appeal.
2. C02 precision pen and wash — strongest operational default.
3. C07 linocut civic portrait — strongest ownable icon and marketplace signature.

C03 scored highly because it is extremely legible and scalable, but it is better as campaign language than as the default portrait. C06 is strategically important as an explanatory format, not as the canonical face. C08 needs a stricter animation-specific iteration because the first export is too close to watercolor.

## System rule

Medium communicates context, not capability or authority. Agent specialty should be communicated through stable geometry, a role-specific artifact silhouette, deterministic skill metadata, accessible descriptions, and real workflow evidence. Do not give every personality a random illustration style; that would weaken recognition and make the constellation look like unrelated generated art.

## Next evidence test

Place C01, C02, and C07 into the same real `.ai` agent-profile layout with identical copy and controls. Run a randomized five-second test for role recognition, warmth, competence, calibrated trust, memorability, and adoption intent. Then test the selected direction across five genuinely different agent roles before scaling to fifty.

The complete heuristic matrix and generation receipts are in `style-evaluation.v1.json`.
