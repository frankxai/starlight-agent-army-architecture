# Phase C status — founder rejected

Frank rejected all ten Phase C candidates on 2026-08-24. No Phase C candidate is recommended for production, UI testing, or further identity refinement.

## Why the study failed

The experiment varied illustration media while locking the same tall adult humanoid. That answered “how can one robot be repainted?” rather than the intended questions:

- Should this agent be full-scale, compact, short-legged, chibi, creature-like, biotech, modular, or non-humanoid?
- What body and movement language makes its specialty legible before any label is read?
- Which embodiment belongs in an operational profile, an Academy lesson, a cinematic story, a marketplace card, or a live runtime graph?
- How should one identity translate across full body, companion, avatar, and motion forms?

The previous face-architecture study remains the stronger visual baseline because it changes structural design instead of surface treatment.

## Asset disposition

The images are preserved for provenance and as negative evidence. They must not appear in production or be presented as finalists. The historical maker scores are non-dispositive and superseded by the founder decision.
