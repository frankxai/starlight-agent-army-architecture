# Founder review status

Review date: 2026-08-29

## Decision

The Phase E–I character and lifecycle batch is **not approved as the next production identity direction**. Frank's feedback is that the earlier v1 Starlight portraits are closer to the intended quality and feeling, and that the newer batch should not replace them.

The files remain in the repository as inspected research and negative evidence. They must not be deleted, silently promoted, used as canonical style references, or integrated into a public site, marketplace pack, plugin, skill pack, or agent pack without a later explicit founder decision.

This decision supersedes maker scores. I04's 26/30 diagnostic score means only that its supervised-workflow composition contains useful information; it does not make the artwork approved.

## What remains valuable

- The morphology breadth proves that Starlight should not use one universal humanoid body.
- Compact specialists, non-humanoid instruments, architectural intelligence, clean biotech, creature companions, and micro-interface forms remain valid categories to redesign.
- I04's maker–verifier–human-gate composition remains a workflow reference after every generated interface is replaced with deterministic overlays.
- The independent critique and risk labels remain useful acceptance tests.

## Next direction

The next pass must be a controlled improvement, not a wholesale aesthetic reset:

1. Preserve the strongest earlier v1 recognition as a selective cinematic continuity lane.
2. Make the primary `.ai` and `.org` system slightly more premium, quieter, more function-shaped, and more grounded in real agent work.
3. Compare full-scale, compact, short-legged, creature-like, biotech, instrument, architectural, and micro-interface embodiments only in contexts where each is useful.
4. Treat eye systems as attention and state instruments. Warmth should come mainly from posture, timing, cooperation, and honest uncertainty—not oversized eyes or simulated human emotion.
5. Generate only after one application quartet is inspected and Frank selects, blends, or restarts the direction.

The executable plan is `asset-program.v1.json`. Until its machine, evidence, and human gates pass, its status remains `held` and production mutation remains false.
